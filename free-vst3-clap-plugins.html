Free VST3 and CLAP plugins

What “free” means here

  • Freeware: mu-Clid MIDI, mu-Toni MIDI and mu-Link are free, with no licence and no reminder.
  • One-off licence: mu-Clid, mu-Tant, mu-Toni, mu-On, mu-Tilate and mu-Tronic are not trials. Nothing is switched off or limited. If you like one, you can buy a licence key at the fixed price in the table, through Lemon Squeezy. The key removes the licence reminder for good.
  • Open source: the source code is GPLv3 on GitHub.
  • No sign-up: download and use them straight away.

The catches

  • Windows only for now. macOS is on hold.
  • The licensed products show a polite licence reminder once each time they load, until you add a licence key.
  • These are creative instruments: sequencers and synths with their own character. They are not general-purpose sample libraries or mastering tools.

Which one to try first?

  • A Euclidean rhythm for the synth you already own: mu-Clid MIDI. See what is a Euclidean sequencer.
  • An arpeggiator that plays a scale or chord for you: mu-Toni MIDI. See what is a generative arpeggiator.
  • A moving, evolving drone: mu-Tant. See what is a wavetable drone synth.
  • A quick techno or house groove: mu-On. See what is a 909-style groove sequencer.
  • Cut up a drum loop: mu-Tilate. See what is a loop slicer plugin.
  • Several of these live on one machine: mu-Link and mu-Tronic.

Questions people ask

Are these plugins really free?

Yes. They are free to download and every feature works. mu-Clid MIDI, mu-Toni MIDI and mu-Link are freeware. The other products have a fixed one-off licence price, and show a polite licence reminder until you add a licence key.

Do they work in my DAW?

They are VST3 and CLAP plugins for Windows, so they work in any DAW that hosts either format. mu-Clid, mu-Tant, mu-Toni and mu-On also run standalone.

Is there a Mac version?

Not at the moment. macOS is on hold.
