A rig of up to 16 layers

μ-tronicOne empty rig. Any layer from the μ-series.

μ-tronic starts empty. Add a layer to any of its sixteen slots, taken from μ-clid, μ-tant, μ-toni or μ-on, and play them together over one tempo with one mixer, buses and scenes. It is free, and it runs on its own, with no DAW needed.

What it does

Build your own
μ-series instrument.

Each μ-series app is one idea. μ-tronic lets you mix them. Put a Euclidean rhythm, a drone, an arpeggio and a kick drum in the same rig, and play them all from one window.

Sixteen empty slots

Start with an empty rig and fill the slots you want. Add a layer to a slot, change its type, clear it. Page 1 shows slots 1 to 8 and page 2 shows 9 to 16.

Slots

Layers from four apps

Pick from the μ-clid rhythm, the μ-tant pattern, the μ-toni arpeggiator, and the μ-on kick, bass, hat, snare and rumble. Each is the same layer you know from its own app, with its own sequencer, modulators and insert effect.

Layer types

One mixer, with buses

Every layer feeds a mixer strip with the same sends and sidechain as the rest of the μ-series. Send layers to one of three buses, each with two insert effects, instead of straight to the master.

Mixer

Bring your presets in

Import a layer from a saved μ-clid, μ-tant, μ-toni or μ-on preset straight into a slot, so sounds you made in the other apps come into your rig.

Import

Scenes

Each layer keeps a bank of clips. Press a scene and clip n launches on every layer at once, so one click changes the whole rig.

Scenes

Built for the stage

It is a standalone app, made for playing live, with the same shared look and controls as every other μ-series app. No DAW needed.

Standalone
A typical start

Three steps to a rig.

Add a layer

Click an empty slot and choose a layer type, such as a kick or a pattern. It appears with its own sound controls and sequencer.

Fill more slots

Add up to sixteen layers. Use the page buttons to move between slots 1 to 8 and 9 to 16, and balance them on the shared mixer.

Press Play

Everything runs on one tempo. Group layers onto buses, add effects, and switch scenes as you play.

A standalone app. μ-tronic is not a plug-in. It has no VST3 or CLAP version and runs on its own, like μ-link.
System requirements

System

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. macOS is on hold, and there is no Linux version yet.

Format

A desktop app that runs on its own. It has no VST3 or CLAP version.

You will need

A sound card or audio interface. A MIDI keyboard or controller is optional.

Download μ-tronic

μ-tronic is free. There is nothing to buy and no key. The source is open under GPLv3, like the rest of the family.
