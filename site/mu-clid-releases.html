μ-clid — what's new

Every change that affects you, version by version. Numbers in brackets are our internal tracking references.

Next release in testing In testing

Improved

  • The demo now plays a single rhythm with patterns of up to 16 steps (saving stays disabled); a licence key unlocks everything. (1119)
  • Modulation targets are shown one at a time, stepped through with up/down arrows; the effects section is now labelled "Effects". (1117)
  • Every dropdown now has the LCD look, labels are engraved into the metal, and the Pitch / Filter / Amp / Insert headings are name plates. (1116)
  • The modulation dropdowns now match the LCD tabs, and the redundant "Mod A" name is gone from the modulation header. (1115)
  • The modulation tabs (A–H, Matrix) now look like small LCD displays, the selected one lit up. (1114)
  • The rhythm list and the modulation section now share the brushed-metal look, with each rhythm and each modulation area in its own raised box. (1113)
  • Empty ring steps are now fully dark, and panel borders look like paint on the metal. (1112)
  • The Euclid A, Euclid B and Accent rows are now named by small engraved plates, and the rows and boxes are evenly spaced. (1111)
  • The rhythm ring is now solid, and each step looks like a dim lamp: hits softly lit, pads faintly lit, empty steps dark. The raised boxes share the panels' brushed-metal finish. (1110)
  • Panels now have a brushed-metal finish, and the Euclid, Pad and Insert boxes sit slightly raised. (1107)
  • Each Euclid row's Steps, Hits and Rotate now sit in their own box, and the Trig/Leg and Poly/Mono switches have moved to the far right. (1106)
  • The centre of the rhythm ring is now a raised, embossed disc, and knobs and switches cast a soft shadow away from the top-right light. (1105)
  • The rhythm ring now has depth — recessed grooves, curved shading, light from the top right and a soft glow on hits — to match the knobs. (1103)
  • Insert Start now shows the actual step the insert begins on, counting from 1 — so with a Pre Pad of 4 it starts at step 5. (1094)
  • Panels now use μ-clid's purple with a soft tint and a gentle highlight in the top-right corner; each rhythm's own colour now outlines its preset bar. (1099, 1100)
  • The Effect, Delay and Reverb send knobs have moved from the Amp panel into the Insert panel, beside the insert type dropdown. (1097)
  • The Pitch envelope Depth knob now sits above Release, making the Pitch panel narrower. (1096)
  • Stepped knobs now show one tick per position around the dial, so you can see how many settings they have.

Fixed

  • Saving a rhythm preset whose name has a dot or a symbol (such as "Kick 1.5" or "A:B") now keeps the whole name. (1146)
  • Modulating Pre Pad or Post Pad now reaches the whole 0–63 range of the knob (it stopped at 12). Existing presets are converted automatically so they sound the same. (1123)
  • The status bar now names insert-effect knobs after the chosen effect — "Compressor Threshold" rather than "Insert Threshold". (1101)
  • Pre Pad, Post Pad and Insert Length can no longer add up to more than the rhythm's steps — together they always leave at least one step for the pattern — and the insert always sits between the Pre Pad and Post Pad gaps. Pre Pad and Post Pad are no longer stuck at a maximum of 12 — they can now use any steps the rhythm has spare. (1093, 1095)
v1.0.0.971 released 2 October 2026 Current

Improved

  • Trig/Leg and Poly/Mono are now slide switches in their own column beside the Euclid knobs, so they no longer look like padding controls. Flipping either explains the setting in the status bar. (1087)
  • The Logic dropdown now sits in the bottom-right corner of the rhythm circle (at the end of the controls row in μ-clid LITE), so Euclid A, Euclid B and Accent are evenly spaced. (1086, 1089)
  • The Pad / Mute toggles are now small slide switches beside their knobs, so the Pad and Insert knobs are now full size (the same as Steps, Hits and Rotate) and their labels are no longer cut off. Flipping a switch shows what that setting does in the status bar. (1084, 1090)
  • New knob design throughout — raised dials with a glowing ring and a single dot marking the position; stepped controls show their value in the centre. Knob labels no longer carry units (the status bar shows them as you turn).
v1.0.0.926 released 17 June 2026

New

  • The plugin now tells you when a newer version is available — a banner appears along the bottom showing your version and the latest one, click it to go to the download page. (1026)

Improved

  • Settings controls are now left-aligned and indented under their headings, with a Contact Support link at the bottom. (1031)

Fixed

  • Fixed a potential click/dropout when using modulation — the modulation engine no longer allocates memory on the audio thread. (1039)
  • Fixed a potential click/dropout when automating reverb or effect parameters — these no longer allocate memory on the audio thread. (1040)
  • The in-plugin update check now runs once per session instead of on every window open. (1038)
  • Aligned the Close button on the MIDI program-change preset tables. (1032)
  • Fixed garbled characters in the MIDI program-change preset table headings and in the "sample missing" messages. (1014)
  • In Large window size, the master-loop step counter ("12 / 32") was cut off in the transport bar — now shows in full. (1009)
v1.0.0.865 released 13 June 2026

New

  • μ-clid LITE now has a Normal / Large window-size selector, so you can scale the plugin window. (989)

Improved

  • In a DAW, the transport play button now turns into a stop icon while the host is playing — matching the standalone. (994)
  • Transport bar and LITE layout polish: clearer play/stop state, tidier spacing, labels and background. (990, 991)

Fixed

  • Fixed knobs accidentally changing value when you scroll over them in a DAW (e.g. while scrolling the timeline). (988)
  • Fixed μ-clid sometimes failing to open or crashing on launch when an older activation file was present. (995)
v1.0.847 released 12 June 2026

New

  • μ-clid LITE is now completely free — no licence required, no time limit. (985)
  • Demo mode: try the full plugin with a single track; saving is disabled until you activate a licence. (981, 982)
  • The sidechain input now appears in CLAP hosts such as Bitwig, matching VST3. (969)

Improved

  • New modulators now start in Unipolar mode. (968)
  • The modulator step grid now lines up with the actual step length. (963, 967)
  • Double-click a modulator node to delete it. (962)
  • Modulation curves no longer overshoot the visible range when bending. (957)
  • The master meter and fader now reflect the master insert effects. (965)

Fixed

  • External (DAW) sidechain now ducks correctly — previously it could produce no ducking at all. (974)
  • Mixer channel strips no longer show stale dimming after loading a multi-track preset. (973)
  • μ-clid LITE no longer crashes when reopening a saved DAW project. (971)
  • Fixed a possible crash at high sample rates (88.2 kHz and above) in the Chorus effect. (961)
