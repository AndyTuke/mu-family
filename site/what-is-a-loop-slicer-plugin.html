Guide · Loop slicers

What is a loop slicer plugin?

A loop slicer cuts an audio loop, usually drums, into small pieces so you can play them back in a different order.

How slicing works

The slicer looks for transients, the sharp starts of each hit, and puts a cut at each one. A drum loop with sixteen hits becomes sixteen slices. Each slice can then be moved, repeated, reversed, left out, re-pitched or filtered on its own.

Why it is useful

  • Turn one loop into many new grooves.
  • Make fills, stutters and glitches in seconds.
  • Match a loop to your project tempo.
