μ-tant — what's new

Every change that affects you, version by version. Numbers in brackets are our internal tracking references.

Improved

  • New Sync offset in Settings (MIDI Clock section, shown when the clock source is MIDI In): enter your audio output latency (about 20–40 ms) and the app plays that much earlier, so it lands on the beat with a hardware master. Saved between sessions. (1253)
  • New Time signature choice in Settings (Transport): the position display counts in 2/4 to 12/8 instead of always 4/4. Patterns still run in four-beat bars. (1295)
  • Apps connected to μ-Link now respond about twice as fast to Stop, mutes, knob moves and preset changes (about 85 ms behind instead of about 170 ms), and your MIDI keyboard feels the same shorter delay. (1256)
  • The BPM field now takes a tenth of a BPM (20.0–300.0, e.g. 127.5): the arrows step a whole BPM, Shift+click steps a tenth, and you can type a value. μ-Link’s tempo control does the same. (1296)
  • MIDI clock sync in the standalone is much tighter: the beat now follows the external clock smoothly instead of in steps, so patterns and gates line up with a hardware master within a fraction of a millisecond (they could land up to a clock tick late, about 20 ms at 120 BPM, and wobble with the clock). The first clock after Start now counts as the downbeat, as the MIDI spec says. (1259)
  • μ-Link slaved to an external MIDI clock now keeps its grid on the master’s downbeat instead of keeping whatever offset it started with and slowly creeping. (1257)
  • MIDI clock sync in the standalone: a new “Clock” lamp in the transport bar shows whether the clock is coming in (amber = waiting, green = locked, red = lost). If the clock stops arriving while playing (a pulled cable, say) the app stops instead of running on, and carries on when the clock comes back. (1252)
  • The MIDI clock “Messages” choices are now named “Clock only”, “Transport only” and “Clock + Transport”. They work as before. (1291)
  • With μ-Link connected, the standalone now hears your MIDI keyboard and controllers (it used to ignore MIDI while connected). For now there is a short delay of about a tenth of a second while connected. (1255)
  • In a DAW, the transport bar now counts bars and beats in the song’s time signature (3/4, 7/8 and so on) and lines up with pickup bars. (1251)
  • MIDI clock sync in the standalone now follows Song Position Pointer: when your hardware sequencer jumps to a new point and continues, the app continues from the same point. (1258)
  • μ-Link’s MIDI clock output is now evenly spaced (it used to arrive in bursts) and also sends Start, Stop, Continue and song position, so outboard gear follows play, stop and rewind. Changing μ-Link’s MIDI output while playing no longer resets the tempo or position. (1254, 1258)
  • In a DAW, the transport display (play button and bar position) now follows the DAW more reliably, including while the plugin is bypassed. (1243)
  • The plugin window uses less CPU: knobs, step editors and animations only redraw when something on screen actually changes, and hidden panels stop animating. (1242)
  • In a DAW, the gate patterns now line up with the DAW’s bars and follow its loop and position jumps (they used to run from wherever they started). (1188)
  • With MIDI clock sync on in the standalone, the clock’s Start and Stop now control playback fully — its Stop stops the app even if you pressed Play. (1188)
  • Presets and saved projects now store each setting's real value (and choices by name), so a future update that changes a knob's range can't shift a saved sound. Older presets and projects still load as before. (1237)
  • Presets and projects saved with this test build won't open correctly in earlier builds — keep a copy if you go back to an older version. (1237)
  • The preset list and the Save dialog open faster, especially with lots of presets or presets that carry embedded samples. (1234)
  • The mixer page starts to take on the metal look: the channel strips and the effects rack sit on screwed metal panels, and each effect row is in its own raised box. The knob-size test knobs are gone. (1181, 1182)
  • The gate pattern editor now has the metal look: its controls sit in a raised box and the envelopes light up on a screen set into the panel, like the modulator display. (1179)
  • Every knob on the main page is now μ-Tant green. (1178)
  • Each oscillator's level is now a Level knob at the end of its own panel, and the noise level sits next to a smaller noise-type selector; the separate Mixer box is gone, giving the filter more room. (1175, 1176, 1177)
  • The status bar at the bottom now shows each control's name and value as you hover over it or turn it, as in μ-Clid. (1165)
  • Screwed metal panels like μ-Clid's: each voice section (oscillators, X-Mod, filter, effects, mixer) sits in its own raised box with its name on a plate above it, the panels have cross-head screws in their corners, and the modulator display is taller. (1163)
  • New colours: the panels look like green paint on brushed metal (darker and less glowing), and each voice has its own colour that lights its sidebar icon, its name and its preset selector. (1147)
  • The voice preset list is now sorted by name. (1138)
  • New look: brushed-metal panels in μ-Tant green, LCD-style dropdowns and tabs, engraved labels and name plates, matching μ-Clid. (1120)
  • The demo now plays a single voice with patterns of up to 16 steps (saving stays disabled); a licence key unlocks everything. (1119)
  • Knobs cast a soft shadow away from the top-right light, so they sit up off the panel.
  • Stepped knobs now show one tick per position around the dial, so you can see how many settings they have.

Fixed

  • MIDI clock sync in the standalone: with Messages set to “Clock only” the Play button now starts the app (it used to stay stopped), and with “Transport only” the app now plays at its own BPM instead of sitting on the first step. (1261)
  • Loading a voice preset saved before some settings existed now puts those settings to their defaults, instead of keeping the previous voice's values. (1237)
  • Presets are grouped under their categories in the preset list again, the preset browser shows each preset's category and description, and Save fills in the current preset's category. (1233)
  • A preset saved with Save → Save as Default is now restored when μ-Tant opens, as in μ-Clid. (1231)
  • When following external MIDI clock, the tempo is now right from the first beat after Start — it used to run fast (or at the previous tempo) for about a beat. (1232)
  • If a preset can't be saved (for example a full disk or a read-only folder) you now get an error message instead of nothing, and an interrupted save can no longer leave a half-written preset. (1230)
  • Modulating X-Mod Index, X-Mod Depth or X-Mod SSB now moves them by the depth you set — they were reacting about 100 times too strongly. (1121)
  • The status bar now names insert-effect knobs after the chosen effect — "Compressor Threshold" rather than "Insert Threshold". (1102)

v1.0.0.926 released 17 June 2026

New

  • Master Loop control in the transport bar (like μ-clid) — pick a loop length and see the step counter. (1010)
  • Hot-swap timing setting — choose whether preset / program-change swaps land on the master loop or on each voice's own loop, for musically-timed changes. (1011)
  • External MIDI clock sync (standalone) — slave μ-tant's tempo and transport to incoming MIDI clock. Set it under Settings → MIDI Clock. (1012)
  • Note mode — play μ-tant like an instrument: the drone sounds only while a MIDI note is held, and the held note sets the pitch (works in a DAW and from a keyboard in standalone). Switch it on under Settings → Note Mode. (1012)
  • Redesigned X-Mod — two lanes instead of three fixed knobs. Lane A: one Index knob with FM / PM / TZFM modes, plus Sync and Feedback. Lane B: a bipolar Depth knob with AM, RM and SSB (single-sideband frequency-shift) modes — from tremolo-like amplitude mod through clangorous ring mod to shimmering inharmonic drones. Existing presets are carried over automatically. (1019)
  • The plugin now tells you when a newer version is available — a banner appears along the bottom showing your version and the latest one, click it to go to the download page. (1026)
  • 100 new factory voice presets (drones, pads, basses, leads, textures, rhythmic) and 100 full presets combining them with effects — in the Voices and Presets browsers. (1029)
  • All filter controls — cutoff, resonance, drive and low-cut — for both filters are now modulation destinations, grouped as Filter 1 and Filter 2 in the destination list. (1030)
  • Pitch-envelope depth (per oscillator) and filter-envelope depth (both filters) are now modulation destinations too. (1034)

Fixed

  • Fixed a potential click/dropout when using modulation — the modulation engine no longer allocates memory on the audio thread. (1039)
  • Fixed a potential click/dropout when automating reverb or effect parameters — these no longer allocate memory on the audio thread. (1040)
  • The in-plugin update check now runs once per session instead of on every window open. (1038)
  • Aligned the Close button on the MIDI program-change preset tables. (1032)
  • Fixed garbled characters in the MIDI program-change "Full Presets" table heading. (1014)
  • Fixed the filter envelope (gate editor's FILT layer) barely moving the cutoff — it now follows the drawn shape and sweeps the filter as intended. (1020)
  • Pitch now glides smoothly — the pitch envelope and smooth modulators sweep continuously instead of stepping in semitones. Step modulators still snap to semitones so you can write melodies. (1021)
  • Step modulators now snap their drawing grid to the target's musical units — an octave target gives 3 steps up/down, a scale-degree target 12 — so you draw notes directly instead of guessing heights. (1022)
  • Octave is now a step-only modulation target — it's hidden when a modulator is in Smooth mode, since a continuous octave glide isn't musical. (1023)

Improved

  • The factory presets have been completely reworked — every voice now has its own evolving gate pattern (no more flat held drones), the rhythms are more musical and leave space so layered voices play off each other, and the combined presets pair voices that complement one another. (1042)
  • Switching to a preset with fewer voices while playing now fades the removed voices out smoothly, so their filter/effect tails ring out instead of cutting off. (1013)
  • The Settings page has been restyled to match μ-clid — grouped General and MIDI sections with clear headings, dividers and a consistent layout. (1004)
  • Settings controls are now left-aligned and indented under their headings, with a Contact Support link at the bottom. (1031)
  • Oscillator knobs reordered — the Pitch Env knob now sits with the pitch controls (Oct / Semi / Fine) and wavetable Position is last, so it no longer reads as a "position envelope". (1033)
  • The modulator destination list now hides the Insert effect targets when no insert effect is selected. (1035)
v1.0.0.865 released 13 June 2026

Improved

  • In a DAW, the transport play button now turns into a stop icon while the host is playing — matching the standalone. (994)
  • Transport bar polish: clearer play/stop state and tidier spacing and labels. (990, 991)

Fixed

  • Fixed a rare crash on launch that could occur when a saved online-activation file was present. (995)
  • Fixed knobs accidentally changing value when you scroll over them in a DAW (e.g. while scrolling the timeline). (988)
v1.0.847 released 12 June 2026

New

  • Demo mode: try the full synth with a single voice; saving is disabled until you activate a licence. (981, 982)
  • The sidechain input now appears in CLAP hosts such as Bitwig, matching VST3. (969)

Improved

  • New modulators now start in Unipolar mode. (968)
  • The modulator step grid now lines up with the actual step length. (963, 967)
  • Double-click a modulator node to delete it. (962)
  • Modulation curves no longer overshoot the visible range when bending. (957)
  • The master meter and fader now reflect the master insert effects. (965)

Fixed

  • External (DAW) sidechain now ducks correctly — previously it could produce no ducking at all. (974)
  • Fixed a possible crash at high sample rates (88.2 kHz and above) in the Chorus effect. (961)
