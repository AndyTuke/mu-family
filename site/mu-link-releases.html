v1.1.0.1415 released 17 October 2026 Current

New

  • μ-link is a free desktop app for Windows that connects your μ-series standalone apps. It sums their audio to one output and keeps them all on one sample-accurate master clock.
  • A performance mixer with a strip for each connected app, up to eight. Each strip has a four-knob EQ, a fader, stereo meters, mute, solo and a Dropouts lamp.
  • A master strip with two insert effects and a safety limiter.
  • Eight scenes that send program changes to your connected apps, so you can switch presets across the whole rig in one click.
  • Follow an external MIDI clock, or send MIDI clock out to outboard gear, with Start, Stop, Continue and song position.
  • Choose Low, Normal or High latency to suit your computer.
