Release Notes

μ-toni — what's new

Every change that affects you, version by version. Numbers in brackets are our internal tracking references.

Next release in testing In testing

New

  • Root and Scale are now one key and scale selector: a small display (for example "C Minor") that opens a panel with a mini keyboard and the list of scales. The Root knob no longer shows a modulation ring. (1827)
  • Eight new inserts in every layer's insert menu: Auto Pan, Gate, Waveshaper (valve-style, with Bias and Sag), Comb, Freq Shift, Formant (vowel sounds), Stutter (tempo-locked repeats) and Lo-Fi (wobble, noise, crackle and tone).
  • Tremolo and Auto Pan tempo sync now runs 1/64, 1/32, 1/16, then 2 to 64 sixteenths (up to four bars).
  • The Flanger and Phaser inserts can now sync to tempo, on the same scale.

Improved

  • The Gate insert now shows the incoming level as a green meter around its Threshold knob, so you can set the threshold by eye. (1825)
  • Tape Sat is rebuilt as a tape-style saturation, with a new Head Bump knob and oversampling.
  • The Chorus, Flanger and Phaser inserts now use the same richer versions as the sends. Chorus has three voices. Existing patches that use these inserts will sound different.
  • The status bar now describes the Tape Sat Head Bump knob.

Fixed

  • The Rate knob on the slow-rate inserts (Flanger, Phaser, Tremolo, Auto Pan and Chorus) now turns smoothly. Its first notch used to jump to 0.2 Hz; it now steps in 0.01 Hz.
v1.2.0.1490 released 15 November 2026 Current

New

  • MIDI mapping editor: Settings, MIDI Mapping… lists your controller mappings. Press Add mapping, move a knob, fader or pad on your controller, then choose what it does (a parameter, Play/Stop, mute, solo or select a layer, clips, presets, panic). Learn gives up after ten seconds or on Esc. (1325)
v1.2.0.1489 released 15 November 2026

New

  • Bounce to WAV: Settings now has a Bounce to WAV button. Pick a length, a tail so effects can ring out, a sample rate and a file, and the app renders what you have built to a 24-bit WAV, with the live transport paused while it works. (1772)

Improved

  • The whole family moves to version 1.2. Under the hood, the shared engine has been rebuilt on a faster, more flexible foundation. No change is intended to how your sessions, presets or sounds behave; if you hear a difference, please tell us.
  • The modulation target list is tidier: each section of targets now appears once under its own heading, and each layer type lists its own targets. (1771)
  • Licensing is simpler. The download is free with every feature. A polite licence reminder shows every time an instance loads; Maybe later closes it for that instance only. A one-off £20 licence removes it, and existing licence keys stay valid. Settings also has a Send Feedback link. (1803)
  • μ-host is free with any install and lists only the engines you have licensed. In free mode it explains that it is a lightweight single-channel host.
v1.1.0.1484 released 9 November 2026

New

  • New insert effects: Chorus, Flanger, Phaser, Pitch Delay and Tremolo. Pitch Delay is a delay whose repeats are pitch-shifted from −12 to +12 semitones. Tremolo can be sine, triangle or square, has an auto-pan phase control, and both can sync to tempo. Find them in the insert slots. (1776, 1777, 1778)

Improved

  • The status bar now shows the name and value of the last control you touched, for every knob, dropdown, number box, switch, button and slider. Some controls, notably in mu-On, used to leave it blank. (1782)
v1.1.0.1458 released 7 November 2026

Fixed

  • Clicking or dragging layers in the sidebar could crash the app. That is fixed. (1767)
  • Loading a preset while the plugin is bypassed in your host now takes effect at once, so un-bypassing no longer makes the sound jump. (1715)
  • Turning an insert’s Tone knob to its fully open end no longer clicks as the filter switches in and out. (1702)
  • The Karplus insert’s feedback is now limited more gently, so high feedback settings ring on a sustained note without harsh clipping. (1714)
v1.1.0.1452 released 27 October 2026

Improved

  • Insert effect parameters now appear in the modulation destination list for every layer, and follow the insert effect you choose.
v1.1.0.1451 released 26 October 2026

New

  • New Tape Delay insert effect: a tape echo with Time, Feedback, Mix and Wear. Each repeat gets darker and softer, and Wear adds tape wobble and saturation. Turn Feedback past 100 % to let it self-oscillate. Time glides when you change it, so turning it bends the echoes like a tape machine. All four knobs can be modulated. Find it in the insert slots.
  • New Overdrive insert effect: a warm, tube-style drive with Drive, Output, Bias and Tone controls. Find it in the insert slots. Presets made before it keep the insert they had. (1650)
  • mu-Toni can now modulate the Amp, Filter and Pitch envelope times (attack, decay, sustain and release). (1651)
  • An “Update available” link appears at the right of the status bar when a newer version is out. The app checks once a day, sends nothing about you, and you can turn it off in Settings (Update check). Click it to open the download page. (1612)

Improved

  • The filter Cutoff centres on 640 Hz and the Glide knob gives more travel to short glides. Every knob shows its value with its unit (ms, Hz, %, dB, note names) in the status bar. (1664)
  • The Clipper insert now shows how hard it is clipping, like the Compressor. (1666)
  • The mixer’s sidechain Attack and Release start at 0 and can go down to 0. (1667)
  • The Effect, Delay and Reverb time and rate knobs spend more of their travel on short times. (1664)
  • The Smooth modulator curve now passes through every point without looping or humping past points that sit close together. It is a smooth line that never overshoots its neighbours.

Fixed

  • The mixer’s Pan, Send and Sidechain knobs, Level fader, Mute and Solo, and the Effect and Delay switches now show their name and value in the status bar. (1664)
v1.1.0.1415 released 17 October 2026

Improved

  • The reverb now has a Decay control. The default Hall tail is shorter than before, and older projects are converted to sound the same.
  • The 24 dB band-pass and notch filters now sit at the same level as their 12 dB versions, instead of being louder or quieter.

Fixed

  • Fixed the loudness of the Fold, Soft Clip and Hard Clip effects, and fixed the sidechain ducking, the Compressor and the 12 dB band-pass filter.
  • Retriggering a note during its release no longer drops the envelope to zero first. It now attacks from where the tail had got to, so there is no click.
v1.1.0.1412 released 15 October 2026

New

  • Free to download, with a one-off licence (GBP).
  • New Support window and licence-key entry. The key you get by email, even if you pay £0, removes the Support window. Nothing in the app is limited either way.
  • This is the first build of μ-toni with licensing.
v1.1.0.1411 released 15 October 2026 · replaced by v1.1.0.1412

New

  • First release of μ-toni, the generative arpeggiator mono-synth. Pick a scale and a chord and it plays the notes through a two-oscillator wavetable synth.
  • Free to download, with a one-off licence. A licence key removes the support prompt; nothing is limited without one.
  • Comes as VST3, CLAP and Standalone for Windows, with Linux in beta.
