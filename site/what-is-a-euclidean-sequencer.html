Guide · Euclidean rhythms

What is a Euclidean sequencer?

A Euclidean sequencer builds a rhythm from just two numbers: how many steps are in the loop, and how many of them should be hits. It then spreads the hits as evenly as it can around the loop.

The idea in one minute

Take 8 steps and 3 hits. You could put the hits next to each other, but a Euclidean rhythm spaces them out as evenly as the grid allows. Written out, with x for a hit and . for a rest:

x . . x . . x .

That is three hits in eight steps, and it is the rhythm musicians know as the Cuban tresillo. Change to 5 hits in 8 steps and you get:

x . x x . x x .

Four hits in sixteen steps is the steady four-on-the-floor kick:

x . . . x . . . x . . . x . . .

Many traditional rhythms from around the world turn out to be Euclidean patterns. The computer scientist Godfried Toussaint pointed this out in a 2005 paper. The method used to space the hits is closely related to Euclid’s old way of finding the greatest common divisor, which is where the name comes from.

Rotation: the third control

The same pattern can start on a different step. Rotating x . . x . . x . by one step gives . x . . x . . x. The hits are still evenly spaced, but they now land off the beat. Steps, hits and rotation together give you a huge number of grooves from three knobs.

Why producers like them

  • Fast. Turn one knob and the whole pattern changes, in a way that still sounds musical.
  • Polyrhythm for free. Run a 5-step and a 7-step loop together and they drift in and out of phase.
  • Good for ideas. It is a quick way to get past a blank grid.

Questions people ask

Is a Euclidean rhythm the same as a Euclidean sequencer?

A Euclidean rhythm is the pattern. A Euclidean sequencer is the tool that makes it: you set steps, hits and rotation and it plays the pattern.

Do I need a DAW?

A Euclidean MIDI effect needs a DAW that can host VST3 or CLAP plugins. mu-Clid, mu-Tant, mu-Toni and mu-On also run on their own as standalone apps.

Is there a free Euclidean sequencer plugin?

Yes. mu-Clid MIDI is freeware for Windows in VST3 and CLAP. mu-Clid is also free to download with every feature working; a licence is optional and you pay what you like.
