v1.1.0.1415 released 17 October 2026 Current

Fixed

  • The rhythm ring no longer hops occasionally when a hit sounds. The ring now reads the step and its position within the step as one consistent reading.
v1.1.0.1410 released 13 October 2026

New

  • μ-clid MIDI now has its own icon, so you can tell it apart from μ-clid in your plugin list and on your desktop. (1461)
  • μ-clid Lite is now μ-clid MIDI, a MIDI-effect product of its own with its own page and download. It is free, with no support message and no key.
  • The installer removes the old μ-clid Lite plugin bundles for you, so you won't end up with two copies.

Improved

  • Your existing projects keep working: only the display name changed. The plugin's internal ID is the same, so a DAW should still recognise it in saved projects.

Fixed

  • A one-step rhythm now keeps sounding instead of playing once and going silent. (1475)

Good to know

  • Some DAWs keep favourites, folders or presets by plugin name. If yours does, you may need to pick the plugin again under its new name, μ-clid MIDI.
  • The download file is now called mu-Clid-MIDI-Windows.zip (and a Linux beta file, mu-Clid-MIDI-Linux.zip).
