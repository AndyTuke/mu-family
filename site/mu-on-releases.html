v1.1.0.1451 released 26 October 2026 Current

New

  • New Tape Delay insert effect: a tape echo with Time, Feedback, Mix and Wear. Each repeat gets darker and softer, and Wear adds tape wobble and saturation. Turn Feedback past 100 % to let it self-oscillate. Time glides when you change it, so turning it bends the echoes like a tape machine. All four knobs can be modulated. Find it in the insert slots.
  • The Chord engine’s filter has a full attack, decay, sustain and release envelope with a depth control, laid out like the drum filter. Chord steps can be ghost notes too. (1672)
  • Ghost notes for Kick, Hat, Snare and FM Perc: Alt+drag a step down to play it softly. A Ghost knob sets how soft, it can be modulated, and a ghost step is drawn as an outline that fills out as the Ghost level rises.(1669)
  • A filter on Kick, Hat, Snare and FM Perc: LP12, HP12, BP12 or Notch12 with Cutoff and Resonance, plus an attack and decay envelope with a depth control that moves the cutoff on every step. Cutoff, Resonance and the envelope can all be modulated. (1670, 1671)
  • Drag the mouse up or down on a Bass step to move its note up or down through the key’s scale. Shift+drag changes its octave. Alt+drag up accents a step on any engine (Alt+drag down ghosts a drum step). On a Hat, drag up or down switches open and closed. (1665)
  • New Overdrive insert effect: a warm, tube-style drive with Drive, Output, Bias and Tone controls. Find it in the insert slots. Presets made before it keep the insert they had. (1650)
  • An “Update available” link appears at the right of the status bar when a newer version is out. The app checks once a day, sends nothing about you, and you can turn it off in Settings (Update check). Click it to open the download page. (1612)

Improved

  • The key and scale now have their own KEY section next to the engine selector on every engine. (1672)
  • A new Bass sequence starts on the key’s root note. The Bass Tune knob reads Root until you turn it. (1668)
  • Decay, attack, release and cutoff knobs give more of their travel to the short and low settings where you play. (1664)
  • Every knob shows its name, value and unit (ms, Hz, %, dB, notes) in the status bar. (1664)
  • The Clipper insert now shows how hard it is clipping, like the Compressor. (1666)
  • The mixer’s sidechain Attack and Release start at 0 and can go down to 0. (1667)
  • The Effect, Delay and Reverb time and rate knobs spend more of their travel on short times. (1664)
  • The Smooth modulator curve now passes through every point without looping or humping past points that sit close together. It is a smooth line that never overshoots its neighbours.
  • The Accent knob now runs from the plain-step level up to full, so an accented step is never quieter than a plain step. (1625)

Fixed

  • The mixer’s Pan, Send and Sidechain knobs, Level fader, Mute and Solo, and the Effect and Delay switches now show their name and value in the status bar. (1664)
v1.1.0.1415 released 17 October 2026

Improved

  • The reverb now has a Decay control. The default Hall tail is shorter than before, and older projects are converted to sound the same.

Fixed

  • Fixed the loudness of the Fold, Soft Clip and Hard Clip effects, and fixed the sidechain ducking, the Compressor and the 12 dB band-pass filter.
v1.1.0.1412 released 15 October 2026

New

  • Pay What You Want, from £0 and suggested £20 (GBP).
  • New Support window and licence-key entry. The key you get by email, even if you pay £0, removes the Support window. Nothing in the app is limited either way.
  • This is the first build of μ-on with licensing.
v1.1.0.1411 released 15 October 2026 · replaced by v1.1.0.1412

New

  • First release of μ-on, the 909-style groove sequencer. Kick, bass, hat, snare and rumble tracks on a 16-step grid, with bass-to-kick sidechain.
  • Pay what you want, from £0. A licence key removes the support prompt; nothing is limited without one.
  • Comes as VST3, CLAP and Standalone for Windows, with Linux in beta.
